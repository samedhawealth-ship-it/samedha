Services

Four disciplines. One advisory relationship.

Most firms make you stitch together a wealth manager, a deal advisor, a bookkeeper and a reporting analyst yourself. Samedha runs all four from the same desk, so decisions in one area account for the other three.

01 — Wealth Management

Wealth Management

Portfolio construction and goal-based planning built around your actual liquidity needs, time horizon and risk appetite — not a fixed model portfolio. We coordinate with your accountant so tax position and cash flow inform every allocation decision.

  • Investment strategy & portfolio construction
  • Retirement and multi-generational wealth planning
  • Risk profiling and periodic rebalancing
  • Consolidated reporting across asset classes and custodians
02 — Corporate Advisory

Corporate Advisory

Fundraising, M&A support, restructuring and capital strategy for founders and boards moving through growth, transition or a transaction. We prepare the numbers a counterparty will actually diligence — because we're the ones who keep them.

  • Fundraising & investor readiness
  • M&A support and transaction structuring
  • Restructuring and turnaround planning
  • Valuation and capital strategy
03 — Accounting

Accounting

Monthly bookkeeping, payroll and statutory compliance run to a standard that stays audit-ready year-round, not patched together in the weeks before filing. Your books close on a schedule, every month.

  • Bookkeeping and monthly close
  • Payroll processing and statutory filings
  • GST / tax compliance and reconciliations
  • Controllership and process design
04 — Financial Reporting

Financial Reporting

Statutory financial statements, board packs and MIS dashboards that translate raw ledgers into a document your investors, lenders and directors can act on — on time, every reporting cycle.

  • Statutory financial statement preparation
  • Management information system (MIS) reporting
  • Board packs and investor reporting
  • Budgeting, forecasting and variance analysis
