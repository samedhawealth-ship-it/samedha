About Samedha

Beyond Compliance. Towards Value.

Samedha Wealth & Advisory Pvt. Ltd. is a Nepal-based wealth management, financial and business advisory firm established on 2083 Shrawan 01, built on a simple belief "financial expertise should do more than just meet compliance requirements. In fact it should help businesses make better decisions and create lasting value.

What we stand for and what we do is what we love to do

Our approach goes beyond simply reporting what has already happened. We seek to understand the business behind the numbers, identify opportunities and risks, challenge assumptions, and translate financial information into decisions that can improve performance and support sustainable growth.

What We Believe

Whether it is strengthening a finance function, evaluating an investment opportunity, developing a financial model, improving business performance, navigating tax and regulatory matters, or planning long-term wealth, we aim to be more than a service provider — we aim to be a trusted financial partner.

Independence

Our advice is priced on its own — never tied to commissions on products we recommend.

Discretion

Client information is handled under strict confidentiality, in line with regulatory requirements.

Rigor

Every report and recommendation is reviewed twice before it reaches a client.

Continuity

You work with a named advisor and team, not a rotating call center.
